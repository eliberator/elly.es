0/5

When they ask: “Do you have any questions for us?”

Never say no. Pick three of these — each one makes you sound like you already work there.

Ask 01

“What does a typical caseload look like for an administrator on the EMO team, and how are cases distributed?”

Shows you are already visualising yourself doing the job.

Ask 02

“Which systems and tools does the team use for case tracking? And how is the initial training structured?”

Signals you take the training promise seriously and plan for ramp-up.

Ask 03

“What does success look like in the first 90 days of this role?”

Turns the interview into a performance conversation — memorable.

Ask 04

“How do the morning and afternoon shift rotations interact? Do teams rotate together?”

Practical interest in teamwork, not just in the schedule.

Ask 05

“How do administrators typically grow here — toward case management, quality, or team coordination?”

Confirms ambition pointed inside their org chart.

Ask 06

“What is the most common failure point in a new admin's first weeks — and what separates those who get past it?”

A mature question that invites the interviewer to coach you already.
