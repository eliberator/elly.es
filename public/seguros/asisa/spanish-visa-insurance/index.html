04 — Cybersecurity for Expats

Protect your identity before you hit send.

The risk

Raw passport and identity-document scans sent over unsecured networks are primary targets for identity theft, fraudulent contracts and financial fraud. Once leaked, your ID can be used to open accounts, sign rentals or contract loans in your name.

Why your Passport / NIE is requested

ASISA's official quoting and policy-issuance portal requires an official ID number to generate legally binding visa certificates. That — and only that — is why your document number is requested. It is typed into a local form, never uploaded here.

This website intentionally contains NO file-upload inputs. You will never be asked to attach documents here — a deliberate barrier against identity theft.

The SaferLayer Protocol

Watermark your ID scans — always.

  1. 1

    Take a photo or scan of your passport / NIE.

  2. 2

    Open saferlayer.com and apply a diagonal watermark before attaching it to any email.

  3. 3

    Use this exact watermark text so the scan is worthless to anyone else:

COPY EXCLUSIVELY FOR ASISA VISA POLICY - AGENT ELLY - [DATE] - NOT VALID FOR ANY OTHER PURPOSE

Open saferlayer.com

Replace [DATE] with today's date before sending (format DD-MMM-YYYY, e.g. 05-Jan-2026).

A watermarked scan can still be verified by ASISA, but cannot be reused to impersonate you anywhere else.

05 — Independent Verification

Don't trust me. Verify me.

Every insurance distributor in Spain is registered with the DGSFP — the Spanish Ministry of Economy's Dirección General de Seguros y Fondos de Pensiones. Check my official authorization yourself, in under two minutes.

DGSFP verification — step by step

  1. 1

    Open the DGSFP official public registry

    Visit the Spanish Ministry of Economy's Dirección General de Seguros y Fondos de Pensiones consultation portal (PUI).

    dgsfp.mineco.gob.es — Registro de distribuidores
  2. 2

    Access the administrative registry

    Enter the Registro administrativo de distribuidores de seguros search form.

  3. 3

    Search under Exclusive Agents for ASISA

    In the Nombre / Razón Social field, type and search among Agentes exclusivos linked to ASISA.

  4. 4

    Confirm active status & exclusivity

    Verify the record shows an active license status and exclusivity with ASISA before sharing any personal detail.
